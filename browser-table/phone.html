Folk phone table

Mount this phone looking straight down at your table, with the printed cards (print them here) in view. The camera stands in for Folk's camera; this screen stands in for its projector — AirPlay-mirror it to a TV.

For an exact table mapping, lay the four printed corner tags where you want the table's corners, then tap calibrate. Without it, the camera frame is assumed to be a straight-down view of the whole table.
