Folk phone table — printable tags

Print this page at 100% scale and cut along the dashed lines. Each card's red ▲ is its nose — the direction the card "points", exactly like the red nose on the mouse table's cards.

The four corner tags are for calibration: lay them where you want the table's corners (TL = top-left of the table, and so on), tap calibrate on the phone, then take them off and play.
